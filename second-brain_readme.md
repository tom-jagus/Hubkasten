# 🧠 second-brain

Welcome to your **second brain** — a personal, structured knowledge base using a modernized Zettelkasten-inspired system.

This folder is designed to help you:

- Think clearly
- Work intentionally
- Create consistently
- Track your life and learning in a durable, extensible system

---

## 📁 Folder Structure and Purpose

### `00 hubs/` ✅ (Top priority)

This is the **table of contents** for your second brain.  
It contains "hub notes" that act like a semantic map — a manually curated, hierarchical, and linkable outline of all your knowledge.

**Subfolders:**

- `core/`: Broad domains of knowledge (e.g. `technology`, `health`, `business`)
- `parent/`: Major categories under each core domain
- `topic/`: Narrow, atomic concepts linked to individual notes

> **Why it's first**: Everything else links back to this structure. It’s the front door of your second brain.

---

### `01 workspace/`

This is your **active working layer** — where you think, plan, reflect, and create.

**Subfolders:**

- `journal/`: Daily or weekly reflections
- `todos/`: Task lists and future planning
- `creative/`: Drafts, original writing, visual thinking
- `logs/`: Work tracking, reviews, time-based activity records

> **Why it comes next**: This is where thinking happens before content becomes permanent.

---

### `02 notes/`

This is your **permanent knowledge base** — a flat folder of atomic, well-formed notes created from workspace reflections or source inputs.

Each note is:

- Conceptual, not contextual
- Standalone and linkable
- Connected to one or more `hub` notes

> **Why it's here**: Notes are the result of thought, not the raw material or the workflow.

---

### `03 sources/`

This is your **input layer** — where you store annotated references from outside your brain.

**Subfolders:**

- `articles/`: Web articles, blog posts
- `books/`: Highlights or summaries from books
- `papers/`: Research papers or academic references
- `podcasts/`: Audio content summaries or notes
- `videos/`: YouTube, lectures, and visual media

> **Why it's here**: Sources exist to support thinking — not as a destination.

---

### `99 misc/` 🗃️ (Final position)

This is your **utility zone**, used for tools and support files.

**Subfolders:**

- `templates/`: Markdown templates for hubs, notes, sources, daily logs, etc.
- `attachments/`: Embedded images, PDFs, diagrams
- `tools/`: Scripts and automations (e.g. `update_hubs.py`)

> **Why it's last**: It supports the system without being part of the core flow.

---

## ✅ Why This Structure Works

- **Semantic structure via hubs** gives you manual control of content relationships
- **Workspace separation** allows you to think and work without cluttering permanent storage
- **Flat permanent notes** support atomic linking and Zettelkasten principles
- **Source separation** ensures clean input-to-output workflows
- **Sorted by priority** so the system guides you, not the other way around

---

## 💡 How to Use This

1. Start each day in `01 workspace/journal/`
2. Capture tasks in `01 workspace/todos/`
3. Draft new ideas in `creative/`, then promote to `02 notes/`
4. Use `03 sources/` to feed your thinking — not to hoard content
5. Curate your knowledge graph through `00 hubs/`
6. Maintain templates and scripts in `99 misc/`

---

> Build your system to think **in context**, link **with intent**, and reflect **in motion**.
