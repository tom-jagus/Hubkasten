# Health

## Description

The **Health** hub organizes knowledge related to physical, mental, and emotional well-being — including practices, systems, strategies, and science aimed at maintaining or improving personal health.

This covers topics such as medicine, nutrition, exercise, sleep science, mental health, preventative healthcare, stress management, and holistic wellness approaches.  
It also includes habits and interventions for longevity, resilience, fitness optimization, and emotional regulation.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Exercise programs, fitness methodologies, and sports science
- Nutrition principles, diets, supplementation, and food science
- Sleep optimization strategies and circadian rhythm management
- Mental health practices like therapy, mindfulness, cognitive behavioral techniques
- Disease prevention, vaccinations, and medical advancements
- Personal health tracking and improvement systems
- Emotional intelligence, stress management, and burnout recovery

This hub distinguishes itself from `[[core/science]]` (which focuses on general scientific discovery) and `[[core/lifestyle]]` (which addresses broader life habits not solely aimed at health outcomes).
