# Politics

## Description

The **Politics** hub captures knowledge related to governance systems, power structures, civic engagement, public policy, lawmaking, and the mechanisms by which societies organize, govern, and regulate themselves.

This includes topics such as political philosophy, government forms (democracy, monarchy, authoritarianism, etc.), policy development, legal frameworks, voting systems, political ideologies, activism, civic duty, public administration, and geopolitical dynamics.  
It also explores how political structures interact with economics, culture, technology, and international relations.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Systems of governance and political organization
- Lawmaking processes, judicial structures, and legal theory
- Political ideologies and their historical development
- Public policy design, implementation, and analysis
- Rights, citizenship, and civic responsibilities
- Power dynamics within and between nations
- Intersections between politics and fields like technology, health, and culture

This hub focuses on **organized human power systems**, distinct from `[[core/culture]]` (which addresses broader societal traditions) and `[[core/business]]` (which deals with markets and value creation).
