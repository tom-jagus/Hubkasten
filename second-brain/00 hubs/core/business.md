# Business

## Description

The **Business** hub covers all knowledge related to the creation, management, operation, and scaling of organizations designed to generate value — whether financial, social, or intellectual.

This includes topics such as entrepreneurship, startups, corporate structures, leadership, marketing, operations, strategy, market analysis, and the future of work.  
It also touches on adjacent areas like productivity systems within businesses, client management, financial planning for enterprises, and the evolving nature of workforces in the digital economy.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Building and running companies
- Business models and frameworks
- Personal branding and freelancing operations
- Organizational leadership strategies
- Project management methodologies
- Innovation and market disruption
- Emerging trends in global work environments

This hub intentionally focuses on **business as a system of value creation**, rather than personal finance (handled under `[[core/finances]]`) or general personal lifestyle (handled under `[[core/lifestyle]]`).
