# Culture

## Description

The **Culture** hub captures all knowledge related to the collective ideas, traditions, values, practices, and institutions that define human societies.

This includes topics like anthropology, history, religion, languages, social norms, rituals, traditions, arts, literature, and the evolution of civilizations.  
It also explores how culture shapes identity, belief systems, social structures, and global interactions over time.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- The history and traditions of specific cultures or civilizations
- Comparative studies of societal development
- Analysis of religious belief systems and their impact on communities
- Language development, symbolism, and cultural storytelling
- Artistic, literary, and performative expressions of cultural identity
- The evolution of social norms and collective memory

This hub focuses on the **descriptive understanding of societies** — how they form, grow, communicate, and transform.  
It is distinct from `[[core/politics]]` (which handles governance and power) and `[[core/philosophy]]` (which focuses on theoretical systems of thought).
