# Science

## Description

The **Science** hub captures knowledge related to the discovery, understanding, and systematic investigation of the natural world through observation, experimentation, and reasoned analysis.

This includes topics such as physics, biology, chemistry, astronomy, earth sciences, environmental science, neuroscience, scientific methodology, and emerging scientific fields like quantum computing and biotechnology.  
It also touches on scientific thinking, critical analysis, research ethics, and the societal impact of scientific advancements.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Natural sciences (biology, chemistry, physics, etc.)
- Applied sciences and technological innovations rooted in science
- Scientific research methods, experimental design, and data interpretation
- Neuroscience, genetics, and cognitive sciences
- Environmental science, sustainability, and planetary studies
- Philosophy of science and the nature of scientific inquiry
- Ethical considerations in scientific development

This hub intentionally focuses on **objective, empirical exploration**, distinguishing it from `[[core/philosophy]]` (which addresses theoretical systems of thought) and `[[core/technology]]` (which handles practical tools and applications derived from scientific knowledge).
