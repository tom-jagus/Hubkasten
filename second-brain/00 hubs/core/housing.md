# Housing

## Description

The **Housing** hub organizes knowledge related to acquiring, maintaining, managing, and optimizing personal living spaces — including homes, apartments, and other forms of residence.

This includes topics such as real estate buying and selling, renting, property investment for personal use, home maintenance and repair, architecture basics, interior design, home security, and residential legal considerations.  
It also touches on broader themes like sustainable living environments, home automation, and strategies for creating functional, healthy, and comfortable living spaces.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Buying or renting property (house hunting, mortgage planning, lease negotiations)
- Home improvement, renovation, and DIY repair techniques
- Real estate market dynamics for personal or small investment use
- Interior design principles for personal spaces
- Sustainable and energy-efficient living strategies
- Smart home technologies and automation systems
- Legal issues related to housing (contracts, tenant rights, insurance)

This hub focuses on **housing from a personal and small-scale perspective**, distinct from `[[core/finances]]` (which handles money planning generally) and `[[core/lifestyle]]` (which addresses broader life activities and habits).
