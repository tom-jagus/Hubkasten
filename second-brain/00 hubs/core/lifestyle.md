# Lifestyle

## Description

The **Lifestyle** hub captures knowledge related to personal habits, activities, hobbies, and choices that shape day-to-day living and overall quality of life.

This includes topics such as cooking, travel, entertainment, sports and leisure activities, personal habits, hobbies, fashion, home life, and broader strategies for living a fulfilling and enjoyable life.  
It also includes frameworks for building sustainable routines, self-improvement systems, and balancing work, health, creativity, and relaxation.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Food, cooking techniques, and culinary exploration
- Travel destinations, trip planning, and cultural experiences
- Entertainment — books, films, music, games, and leisure
- Building and maintaining meaningful hobbies
- Daily and weekly life rituals and routines
- Habit formation and optimization of personal behavior
- Lifestyle design strategies aimed at improving happiness, energy, and creativity

This hub is distinct from `[[core/health]]` (which focuses specifically on physical and mental well-being) and from `[[core/finances]]` (which handles financial management).  
Here, lifestyle emphasizes **how life is lived**, shaped by personal choices and cultural influences.
