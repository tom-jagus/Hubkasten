# Education

## Description

The **Education** hub organizes knowledge related to the systems, methods, philosophies, and practices of teaching, learning, and intellectual growth.

This includes topics such as formal education systems (schools, universities), alternative education models (unschooling, homeschooling), learning psychology, pedagogy, instructional design, skills acquisition, and lifelong learning strategies.  
It also covers meta-learning — the process of improving one's ability to learn and adapt across different domains.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Theories of learning and cognitive development
- Educational frameworks and models (e.g., Montessori, Bloom’s Taxonomy)
- Effective study techniques and memory strategies
- Curriculum development and instructional methodologies
- Adult learning, professional development, and reskilling
- The societal role of education and its evolution over time

This hub focuses on **how knowledge is transferred, developed, and internalized**, distinguishing it from `[[core/science]]` (which addresses objective discoveries) and `[[core/technology]]` (which may involve tools that assist education but are not education themselves).
