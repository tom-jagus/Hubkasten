# Philosophy

## Description

The **Philosophy** hub organizes knowledge related to abstract thinking, reasoning, ethics, metaphysics, epistemology, and frameworks for understanding existence, knowledge, and human behavior.

This includes topics such as classical and modern philosophical systems, theories of mind, moral philosophy, political philosophy, logic and critical thinking, metaphysical models, and reflections on the nature of reality, meaning, and knowledge.  
It also covers approaches to structured reasoning and frameworks for ethical decision-making, belief formation, and life purpose exploration.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Major philosophical traditions (Stoicism, Existentialism, Pragmatism, etc.)
- Ethical frameworks and moral reasoning
- Epistemological questions: How do we know what we know?
- Metaphysical questions: Theories about reality, being, time, and consciousness
- Philosophical analysis of culture, politics, technology, and science
- Practical applications of philosophy for decision-making, leadership, and personal growth
- Logic structures and critical thinking methods

This hub intentionally focuses on **the structure of thought itself**, distinguishing it from `[[core/culture]]` (which deals with societal traditions and practices) and `[[core/science]]` (which deals with empirical investigation).
