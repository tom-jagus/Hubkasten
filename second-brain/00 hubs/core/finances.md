# Finances

## Description

The **Finances** hub captures knowledge related to the management, planning, growth, and safeguarding of personal, family, or small-scale financial systems.

This includes topics such as budgeting, saving, investing, taxation, debt management, insurance, retirement planning, financial independence strategies, and economic literacy.  
It also addresses behavioral finance — understanding how emotions and psychology affect money decisions — and tools for better financial decision-making.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Personal and household budgeting systems
- Investment strategies (stocks, bonds, real estate, crypto)
- Retirement savings plans and financial goal setting
- Managing credit, loans, and debt reduction plans
- Insurance types and risk mitigation
- Principles of financial planning and wealth building
- Economic literacy basics for non-professionals

This hub intentionally focuses on **personal and small-scale financial management** rather than corporate finance or market systems (handled under `[[core/business]]`).
