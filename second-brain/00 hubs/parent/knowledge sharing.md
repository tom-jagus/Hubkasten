# Knowledge Sharing

## Description

The **Knowledge Sharing** hub captures strategies, practices, and experiences related to teaching, mentoring, presenting, and distributing knowledge — whether informally or in structured educational formats.

This includes public speaking, course creation, coaching, mentoring, technical documentation, blogging, curriculum design, and digital content creation.  
It also supports reflection on communication clarity, audience engagement, and knowledge accessibility.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Presentation techniques, feedback, or scripts
- Teaching experiences, curriculum drafts, or lesson plans
- Educational videos, blog posts, or online teaching platforms
- Coaching sessions, mentoring logs, or tutoring strategies
- Tools for knowledge delivery (e.g. slide decks, Notion, LMS)
- Reflections on pedagogy and learner engagement
- Communication models and clarity exercises

This hub connects to `[[core/education]]`, and may overlap with `[[core/workspace]]` (for tools and project management), `[[parent/learning methods & systems]]` (for reversing roles), and `[[core/creativity]]` if you extend your system toward content creation.
