# Mental & Emotional Health

## Description

The **Mental & Emotional Health** hub captures knowledge, reflections, and support systems related to psychological resilience, emotional regulation, self-awareness, and mental well-being.

This includes stress management, anxiety, mood tracking, emotional literacy, therapy notes, meditation, trauma work, and the influence of thought patterns on daily experience.  
It also provides space to document personal insights, mental health tools, journaling habits, and frameworks for responding to emotional triggers or building a resilient mindset.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Mood logs, journaling prompts, and emotional awareness exercises
- Coping strategies for anxiety, overwhelm, or low motivation
- Thought patterns and cognitive distortions
- Mindfulness, meditation, and nervous system regulation
- Therapy insights, models (e.g. CBT, IFS), or session reflections
- Triggers and emotional boundary-setting
- Personal growth reflections around identity, self-worth, and acceptance

This hub connects to `[[core/health]]` and naturally overlaps with `[[core/lifestyle]]`, `[[parent/sleep & recovery]]`, and `[[core/philosophy]]` when exploring selfhood, purpose, or inner dialogue.
