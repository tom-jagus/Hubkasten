# Mind & Consciousness

## Description

The **Mind & Consciousness** hub captures knowledge about mental processes, the nature of subjective experience, personal identity, and the philosophical foundations of cognition and awareness.

This includes philosophy of mind topics such as dualism, materialism, panpsychism, and functionalism; the hard problem of consciousness; mental causation; intentionality; and the mind–body problem.  
It also explores how mental states relate to physical systems (e.g. brains, machines), including questions about free will, self-awareness, and artificial consciousness.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Theories of consciousness: phenomenal experience, qualia, awareness
- The mind–body problem and its proposed solutions
- Mental causation, intentionality, and internal representation
- Personal identity over time: continuity, memory, and change
- Philosophical issues in psychology, neuroscience, and AI
- The nature of thought, belief, emotion, and sensation
- Free will, determinism, and volition

This hub connects to `[[core/philosophy]]`, and often overlaps with `[[parent/knowledge & belief]]`, `[[core/health]]`, `[[core/science]]`, and `[[parent/ethics & morals]]` where issues of personhood and consciousness arise.
