# Legal & Regulatory Matters

## Description

The **Legal & Regulatory Matters** hub captures knowledge about the legal frameworks, obligations, and compliance requirements related to property ownership, renting, development, and land use.

This includes zoning laws, building codes, permits, inspections, rental regulations, landlord-tenant law, insurance policies, tax implications, and legal documentation throughout real estate transactions.  
It also covers interactions with legal professionals, local authorities, and insurance providers, helping you navigate risks and maintain compliance at every stage.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Landlord-tenant legal rights and responsibilities
- Zoning types, code restrictions, and land use classifications
- Permit processes for renovations or development
- Insurance types (e.g., homeowner, liability, title) and claim procedures
- Legal requirements during property sale or lease agreements
- Taxes, exemptions, and documentation (e.g. property tax, capital gains)
- Working with attorneys, inspectors, and government agencies

This hub connects to `[[core/housing]]` and may also intersect with `[[core/finances]]` (for tax and legal cost tracking), `[[parent/renting & tenancy]]`, and `[[parent/homeownership & maintenance]]`.
