# Nutrition & Diet

## Description

The **Nutrition & Diet** hub captures knowledge, tracking systems, and reflections related to how food choices affect health, energy, mood, digestion, and long-term wellness.

This includes nutrient tracking, supplements, food sensitivities, gut health, hydration, eating patterns, blood sugar management, and experiments with dietary protocols (e.g. fasting, keto, elimination diets).  
It also covers food journaling, macronutrient balancing, intuitive eating, and adjustments based on symptoms or medical recommendations.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Food logs, diet tracking tools, and symptom correlations
- Macro/micronutrient insights and common deficiencies
- Supplement plans, stacks, and testing outcomes
- Meal timing strategies (e.g. intermittent fasting, circadian eating)
- Food sensitivities, allergy responses, or gut health issues
- Research on dietary interventions for specific health conditions
- Personal experiments with eating styles and effects

This hub connects to `[[core/health]]` and often overlaps with `[[parent/physical health]]`, `[[core/lifestyle]]` (for routines and preferences), and `[[parent/sleep & recovery]]` (for recovery and energy optimization).
