# Religion & Spirituality

## Description

The **Religion & Spirituality** hub captures cultural and personal systems of belief, practice, ritual, and meaning — including world religions, spiritual movements, sacred texts, and inner philosophical reflection.

This includes notes on comparative religion, historical origins, theological frameworks, symbols, worship practices, moral teachings, spiritual development, and interfaith dialogue.  
It also supports personal explorations of belief, secular spirituality, mindfulness, and ritual behavior outside organized religion.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Major religions (e.g. Christianity, Islam, Buddhism) and their branches
- Belief systems, cosmologies, or moral doctrines
- Rituals, prayer, fasting, and pilgrimage practices
- Spiritual experiences, journaling, or philosophical reflection
- Sacred texts, parables, or commentary
- Religion’s intersection with politics, identity, or art
- Differences and similarities between traditions

This hub connects to `[[core/culture]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/traditions & rituals]]`, `[[core/philosophy]]`, or `[[parent/cultural identity & heritage]]` when belief systems shape personal or group identity.
