# Buying & Selling Property

## Description

The **Buying & Selling Property** hub captures knowledge and workflows related to the process of purchasing or selling real estate — including personal residences, investment properties, and land.

This includes search strategies, home viewings, real estate agent selection, offer negotiation, closing processes, staging and listing, inspections, appraisals, and timing considerations.  
It also includes emotional and financial decision-making, regional comparisons, and checklists or reflections from past property experiences.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- First-time buyer guides, financing prep, and affordability strategies
- Listing checklists, pricing tactics, and home preparation
- Open house scheduling, photography, and staging ideas
- Negotiation tactics, contingencies, and offer timing
- Agent hiring criteria or experience logs
- Selling timelines, legal steps, and closing procedures
- Lessons learned from past purchases or sales

This hub connects to `[[core/housing]]` and may intersect with `[[parent/mortgages & financing]]`, `[[parent/real estate market]]`, and `[[core/finances]]` where budgeting, loans, or return-on-investment come into play.
