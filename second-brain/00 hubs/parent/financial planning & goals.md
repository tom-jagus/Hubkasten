# Financial Planning & Goals

## Description

The **Financial Planning & Goals** hub captures long-term visioning, milestone setting, and actionable strategy for reaching personal and financial life goals — including retirement, large purchases, financial independence, or lifestyle changes.

This includes net worth tracking, life milestones (e.g. home, education, family), early retirement scenarios, income/savings projections, and multi-year planning documents.  
It also supports reviewing and adjusting progress toward big-picture goals and integrating financial decisions with personal values.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Long-term financial goals and timeframes
- Retirement planning and FI/RE scenarios
- Net worth summaries and progress tracking
- Lifestyle cost projections and financial independence milestones
- Major purchase plans (e.g. car, house, education)
- Strategic decision trees and “what if” modeling
- Reflections on financial values, tradeoffs, and motivators

This hub connects to `[[core/finances]]` and heavily overlaps with `[[parent/investing & wealth building]]`, `[[parent/budgeting & expense tracking]]`, and `[[core/lifestyle]]`.
