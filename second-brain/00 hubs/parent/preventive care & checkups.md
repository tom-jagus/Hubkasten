# Preventive Care & Checkups

## Description

The **Preventive Care & Checkups** hub captures records, plans, and knowledge related to proactive health screening, routine assessments, vaccinations, and early detection of potential issues.

This includes general practitioner visits, annual checkups, bloodwork, dental exams, eye exams, skin screenings, vaccination schedules, and age-specific recommendations.  
It also supports tracking due dates, test results, provider experiences, and personal insights that arise from regular contact with healthcare professionals.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Scheduled and completed checkups (e.g. physicals, dental cleanings)
- Lab tests, imaging results, and historical comparisons
- Screening recommendations by age, gender, or condition risk
- Vaccine history and reminders
- Monitoring metrics for early warning signs
- Health maintenance plans or protocols
- Reflections on doctor/patient relationships or systems of care

This hub connects to `[[core/health]]` and often overlaps with `[[parent/medical history & conditions]]`, `[[parent/physical health]]`, and `[[parent/healthcare system & insurance]]`.
