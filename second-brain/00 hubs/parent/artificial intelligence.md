# Artificial Intelligence

## Description

The **Artificial Intelligence** hub covers concepts, models, tools, and practices related to the design and implementation of intelligent systems capable of learning, adapting, and performing tasks that typically require human cognition.

This includes topics such as machine learning, deep learning, neural networks, natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, reinforcement learning, AI ethics, generative models, and large language models (LLMs).  
It also covers the evolving relationship between AI and society, including productivity enhancement, automation, decision support, and the risks and responsibilities associated with deploying advanced AI systems.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Machine learning techniques, frameworks, and architectures
- Training, evaluation, and deployment of AI models
- Applications of AI in fields such as healthcare, finance, and software development
- Natural language models, embeddings, and transformers
- Ethical challenges, explainability, and AI governance
- Generative AI tools (e.g., image synthesis, code generation)
- AI research papers, breakthroughs, and limitations

This hub connects directly to `[[core/technology]]`, and may also be cross-referenced from `[[core/science]]` or `[[core/philosophy]]` when the focus shifts toward theory or ethics.
