# Physics

## Description

The **Physics** hub captures knowledge related to the fundamental principles governing matter, energy, motion, and the forces that structure the universe.

This includes classical mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics, quantum mechanics, relativity, particle physics, and cosmology.  
It also covers experimental and theoretical approaches to understanding the natural laws that underpin reality, as well as applications of physics in engineering, space exploration, and technology development.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Core physical laws and theories (Newtonian mechanics, quantum field theory, general relativity)
- Thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, and heat transfer
- Electromagnetism, wave phenomena, and optics
- Atomic, molecular, and condensed matter physics
- Astrophysics and the physical study of celestial phenomena
- Experimental methods, instrumentation, and lab practices in physics
- Mathematical models and simulations used in physical sciences

This hub connects to `[[core/science]]`, and may also occasionally overlap with `[[core/technology]]` or `[[core/philosophy]]` when discussing theoretical foundations or technological applications of physical principles.
