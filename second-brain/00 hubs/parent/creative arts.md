# Creative Arts

## Description

The **Creative Arts** hub captures traditional and personal forms of artistic expression that communicate cultural meaning through visual, literary, and performative mediums.

This includes painting, sculpture, illustration, photography, poetry, literature, dance, theater, and other analog or hands-on artistic disciplines.  
It also supports reflections on aesthetics, technique, creative philosophy, art history, and cultural movements in the arts.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Art movements and styles (e.g. Impressionism, Surrealism)
- Personal analysis of paintings, poems, or theatrical works
- Techniques and tools used in various creative processes
- Literary devices, storytelling patterns, and poetic structure
- Artist profiles or history of specific creative disciplines
- Cultural or political interpretation of art forms
- Reflections on the meaning, beauty, or purpose of art

This hub connects to `[[core/culture]]` and may also overlap with `[[parent/language & symbols]]` (for metaphors and semiotics), `[[core/creativity]]`, or `[[parent/media & entertainment]]` when works cross into digital formats.
