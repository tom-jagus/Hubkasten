# Media & Entertainment

## Description

The **Media & Entertainment** hub captures cultural content created for mass consumption — including film, music, podcasts, television, streaming, and games — along with the narratives, industries, and analysis behind them.

This includes critical reviews, industry commentary, genre studies, narrative structures, platform trends, and personal reflections on entertainment as cultural influence.  
It also supports note-taking on specific works, creators, performances, technologies, and fandoms within modern entertainment ecosystems.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Movies, series, or soundtracks and your reactions to them
- Music genres, artist profiles, albums, or lyrical breakdowns
- Games, game design, immersive experiences, or esports
- Analysis of media tropes, story arcs, or aesthetic shifts
- Streaming platform comparisons, subscription patterns, or recommendation algorithms
- Podcast summaries or audio storytelling formats
- Reflections on emotional, social, or intellectual impact of entertainment

This hub connects to `[[core/culture]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/pop culture & internet]]` (for celebrity dynamics or fandoms), `[[parent/creative arts]]` (for film/music overlap), or `[[core/technology]]` (for production tools or digital distribution).
