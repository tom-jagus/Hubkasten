# Entrepreneurship & Startups

## Description

The **Entrepreneurship & Startups** hub captures the journey, mindset, and practical mechanics of building new ventures — from idea validation to product-market fit, scaling, and exit.

This includes startup methodologies (e.g. Lean Startup, MVP), funding stages, pitch decks, founder dynamics, and the emotional, financial, and operational challenges of creating a company from scratch.  
It also supports notes on solo ventures, bootstrapping, incubators, accelerators, and lessons learned from startup failures and successes.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Startup phases: idea → validation → launch → growth → scale
- Business plans, elevator pitches, and pitch deck outlines
- MVP design, iteration feedback, and user testing
- Bootstrapping vs VC funding, equity splits, and cap tables
- Founder psychology, resilience, and decision-making
- Tools and platforms for building lean products
- Case studies from accelerators, unicorns, or failed ventures

This hub connects to `[[core/business]]` and often overlaps with `[[parent/business strategy]]`, `[[parent/innovation & disruption]]`, and `[[parent/leadership & management]]`.
