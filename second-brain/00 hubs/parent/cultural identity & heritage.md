# Cultural Identity & Heritage

## Description

The **Cultural Identity & Heritage** hub captures personal and collective expressions of origin, ancestry, and belonging — including ethnicity, nationality, migration history, and intergenerational narratives.

This includes notes about traditions passed down through families or communities, personal reflections on cultural identity, heritage preservation efforts, diasporic experience, and ethnic or indigenous knowledge systems.  
It also supports exploration of cultural pride, assimilation, hybrid identities, and how individuals relate to the broader cultures they inherit or adopt.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Ancestral history, migration stories, and family background
- Reflections on identity, belonging, and cultural duality
- National or ethnic traditions, symbols, or rituals
- Notes on diasporas, language retention, or generational shifts
- Cultural preservation movements or community history
- Tensions between assimilation and authenticity
- Personal journaling on being “from” multiple places

This hub connects to `[[core/culture]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/traditions & rituals]]`, `[[parent/religion & spirituality]]`, and `[[parent/language & symbols]]`.
