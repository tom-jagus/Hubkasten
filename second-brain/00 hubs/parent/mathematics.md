# Mathematics

## Description

The **Mathematics** hub captures knowledge related to abstract structures, numbers, patterns, space, and logical reasoning — the foundational language of science and engineering.

This includes pure mathematics (algebra, geometry, calculus, topology, number theory) and applied mathematics (statistics, optimization, computational mathematics, mathematical modeling).  
It also bridges theoretical work with practical applications in fields such as physics, computer science, finance, engineering, and data science.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Fundamental topics: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry
- Advanced fields: calculus, linear algebra, real analysis, abstract algebra, topology
- Probability theory, statistics, and inferential methods
- Mathematical logic, set theory, proof strategies
- Mathematical modeling of real-world systems (physical, biological, financial)
- Applications of math in machine learning, cryptography, operations research
- History and philosophy of mathematical thought

This hub connects to `[[core/science]]`, and frequently intersects with `[[core/technology]]`, `[[parent/physics]]`, and `[[parent/data systems]]` when applied to scientific or computational problems.
