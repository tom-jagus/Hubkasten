# Travel & Leisure

## Description

The **Travel & Leisure** hub captures knowledge, plans, and reflections related to exploring new places, enjoying downtime, and seeking experiences that recharge, inspire, or entertain.

This includes vacations, cultural trips, weekend getaways, travel logistics, bucket lists, travel gear, and destination research, as well as hobbies and activities meant for pure enjoyment or restoration.  
It also covers intentional leisure practices, scenic experiences, family adventures, solo travel, and ideas for local exploration or seasonal traditions.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Travel plans, itineraries, past trip logs, and lessons learned
- Destination research and comparisons
- Packing lists, tools, booking apps, or travel gear
- Leisure activities like hiking, sightseeing, reading, or relaxing routines
- Bucket lists and "someday" exploration goals
- Cultural experiences, foods, or places of personal significance
- Reflections on travel mindset, rest, and inspiration

This hub connects to `[[core/lifestyle]]` and may intersect with `[[parent/food & cooking]]` (for culinary travel), `[[parent/social life & relationships]]` (shared trips), or `[[core/culture]]` (for deeper engagement with foreign traditions).
