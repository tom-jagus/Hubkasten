# Political Participation

## Description

The **Political Participation** hub captures knowledge about the ways in which individuals and groups engage in political processes, influence governance, and express their civic voice.

This includes voting, campaigning, political activism, public demonstrations, political education, civic technology, citizen assemblies, petitions, grassroots movements, and institutional pathways for engagement.  
It also covers barriers to participation, civic empowerment strategies, and the sociology and psychology behind public involvement in political life.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Voting systems, turnout strategies, and voter suppression
- Political party membership and grassroots organizing
- Activism, civil disobedience, protests, and demonstrations
- Campaigning methods (digital and traditional), volunteering, canvassing
- Civic education and engagement platforms (e.g., town halls, participatory budgeting)
- Legal and social frameworks supporting or inhibiting political involvement
- Public trust, political apathy, and motivations for engagement

This hub connects to `[[core/politics]]`, and may overlap with `[[parent/political communication]]`, `[[parent/public policy & administration]]`, and `[[core/society]]` (if defined) depending on societal context and tools for participation.
