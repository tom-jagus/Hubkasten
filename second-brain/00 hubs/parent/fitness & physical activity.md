# Fitness & Physical Activity

## Description

The **Fitness & Physical Activity** hub captures knowledge and personal systems related to movement, exercise, strength, endurance, flexibility, and overall physical vitality as part of a healthy lifestyle.

This includes strength training, cardio routines, sports, mobility work, stretching, recreational movement, training programs, personal records, and recovery strategies.  
It also covers movement philosophies (e.g. functional training, CrossFit, yoga), progress tracking, injury prevention, and the psychological benefits of physical exertion.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Workout plans, exercise templates, and training programs
- Strength, endurance, flexibility, or mobility goals
- Sports, martial arts, outdoor activities, or recreational movement
- Daily movement goals (e.g. steps, NEAT, posture corrections)
- Recovery tools and routines: stretching, massage, sleep, nutrition
- Reflections on fitness progress and energy levels
- Relationships between movement, mood, confidence, and motivation

This hub connects to `[[core/lifestyle]]` and may overlap with `[[core/health]]` (for medical or rehab-related movement), `[[parent/daily habits & routines]]`, and `[[core/workspace]]` when connected to energy management or focus.
