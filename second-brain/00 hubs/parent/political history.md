# Political History

## Description

The **Political History** hub captures knowledge about the historical development of political systems, ideologies, leadership structures, movements, revolutions, and major events that shaped political landscapes across different eras and regions.

This includes ancient, medieval, modern, and contemporary political evolutions; the rise and fall of empires; key revolutions (e.g., American, French, Russian); decolonization; political reform movements; and the evolution of democratic institutions and global governance models.  
It also covers the biographies of influential political figures and the long-term consequences of historical political decisions.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Major political revolutions, reforms, and regime changes
- Political timelines of nations, empires, and regional unions
- Biographies of historical political leaders, activists, and theorists
- History of democratic institutions, parliaments, constitutions
- Colonialism, decolonization, and post-colonial governance transitions
- War, peace treaties, and geopolitical realignments
- Evolution of political thought and theory over time

This hub connects to `[[core/politics]]`, and naturally overlaps with `[[core/history]]`, `[[parent/political ideologies]]`, and `[[parent/geopolitics & international relations]]` where historical developments influence present-day systems.
