# Language Learning

## Description

The **Language Learning** hub captures methods, materials, and progress tracking related to acquiring new languages — including vocabulary building, grammar, speaking skills, cultural understanding, and fluency milestones.

This includes learning plans, flashcards, immersion strategies, study resources, pronunciation tools, and reflections on learning pace or roadblocks.  
It also supports language-specific notes, practice logs, grammar rules, and evaluations of tools or courses used in the learning journey.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Language goals, timelines, and motivation logs
- Flashcard decks, spaced repetition systems, or vocab lists
- Grammar breakdowns and sentence structure patterns
- Listening, reading, speaking, and writing exercises
- Tools and platforms (e.g. Duolingo, Clozemaster, Italki)
- Cultural insights related to language context
- Milestone tracking: beginner → fluent or CEFR levels

This hub connects to `[[core/education]]` and may intersect with `[[parent/study tools]]`, `[[parent/online learning & courses]]`, and `[[parent/learning methods & systems]]`.
