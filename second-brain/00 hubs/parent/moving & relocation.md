# Moving & Relocation

## Description

The **Moving & Relocation** hub captures knowledge, tools, and personal workflows related to transitioning between homes — whether across the street or to a new country.

This includes packing checklists, change-of-address planning, utility setup, moving company research, downsizing strategies, logistics coordination, and reflections on settling in.  
It also covers emotional, cultural, or psychological aspects of relocation, including adjusting to a new environment, community integration, or routines in temporary housing.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Packing systems, supplies, and labeling methods
- Checklists for moving preparation and post-move setup
- Address changes, subscriptions, and documentation updates
- Movers vs DIY transport options, quotes, and vendor reviews
- Emotional transitions: excitement, anxiety, nostalgia, new beginnings
- International relocation: visas, cost-of-living research, cultural norms
- Lessons learned from past moves or ideal future setup ideas

This hub connects to `[[core/housing]]` and may intersect with `[[parent/homeownership & maintenance]]` (for move-out prep), `[[parent/renting & tenancy]]` (lease transitions), and `[[core/lifestyle]]` for routine reconfiguration.
