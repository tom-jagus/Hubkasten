# Education Policies & Reforms

## Description

The **Education Policies & Reforms** hub captures knowledge, debates, and observations related to the governance, funding, structure, and reform of education systems — at local, national, or global levels.

This includes curriculum standards, school funding models, equity/access discussions, testing mandates, education laws, systemic critiques, and innovation in public or private education.  
It also supports comparative education analysis and reflections on the societal role of education policy.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- National or regional education laws and changes
- Curriculum standardization, testing policies, and accreditation
- School funding models and economic equity issues
- Inclusion policies, accessibility, and special education law
- Homeschooling regulation and alternative education models
- Policy think tank reports or government white papers
- Reflections on education reform movements and outcomes

This hub connects to `[[core/education]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/schooling & achievements]]`, `[[core/politics]]` (for legislative matters), and `[[parent/knowledge sharing]]` (when systems impact teaching practice).
