# Neuroscience

## Description

The **Neuroscience** hub captures knowledge related to the structure, function, development, and disorders of the nervous system — particularly the brain and its impact on behavior and cognition.

This includes fields such as molecular and cellular neuroscience, systems neuroscience, cognitive neuroscience, computational neuroscience, neurophysiology, and neuropsychology.  
It also connects with adjacent domains like artificial intelligence (through brain-inspired computing), mental health research, and neurotechnology.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Brain anatomy, neuronal signaling, and synaptic transmission
- Cognitive processes: perception, attention, memory, language
- Neural network modeling and brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)
- Studies of brain disorders (e.g., Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, depression)
- Functional imaging methods (fMRI, EEG, PET scans)
- Theories of consciousness, decision-making, and emotional regulation
- Intersections between neuroscience, AI, philosophy, and psychology

This hub connects to `[[core/science]]`, and frequently overlaps with `[[core/health]]`, `[[parent/biology]]`, and `[[parent/artificial intelligence]]` (from technology).
