# Daily Habits & Routines

## Description

The **Daily Habits & Routines** hub captures knowledge and personal systems related to structuring your time, behaviors, rituals, and lifestyle for consistency, productivity, health, and balance.

This includes morning and evening routines, habit formation, habit tracking, time-blocking, daily reflection, energy management, and productivity workflows.  
It also covers frameworks for behavior change, automation of tasks, and the psychology of building or breaking habits over time.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Habit stacking, tracking, and behavioral reinforcement
- Morning rituals, evening wind-downs, and time management systems
- Weekly or monthly planning routines and scheduling frameworks
- Daily journaling, gratitude practice, or reflection prompts
- Energy and focus management (e.g., Pomodoro, ultradian cycles)
- Habit change methods (e.g., Atomic Habits, BJ Fogg model)
- Tools or apps used for building and maintaining routines

This hub connects to `[[core/lifestyle]]` and may also intersect with `[[core/health]]` (for wellness-focused habits), `[[core/workspace]]` (for productivity systems), and `[[core/philosophy]]` (for values-aligned living).
