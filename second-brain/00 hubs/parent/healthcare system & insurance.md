# Healthcare System & Insurance

## Description

The **Healthcare System & Insurance** hub captures knowledge, plans, and experiences related to accessing healthcare, navigating insurance policies, managing claims, and interacting with institutions and providers.

This includes understanding coverage types, provider networks, appointment logistics, billing procedures, pre-approvals, reimbursements, and dispute resolution.  
It also supports organizing contact info, benefit summaries, and insights about system design, healthcare inequality, and healthcare quality.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Insurance plans, policies, and benefit summaries
- Claims processes, denials, appeals, and out-of-pocket tracking
- Provider directories, referrals, and appointment systems
- Cost comparisons and service transparency (e.g. lab work, imaging)
- Government vs private system comparisons or research
- Health savings accounts, deductible strategies, and annual planning
- Reflections on healthcare accessibility, quality, or gaps

This hub connects to `[[core/health]]`, and may overlap with `[[core/finances]]` (for budgeting), `[[parent/medical history & conditions]]`, and `[[parent/preventive care & checkups]]`.
