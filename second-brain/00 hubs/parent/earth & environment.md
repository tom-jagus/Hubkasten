# Earth & Environment

## Description

The **Earth & Environment** hub captures knowledge about the physical systems of the Earth and the interactions between natural processes and human activities.

This includes geology, meteorology, oceanography, climatology, environmental science, sustainability practices, conservation efforts, and the study of anthropogenic effects such as climate change, pollution, and habitat loss.  
It also covers natural resource management, ecosystem services, and interdisciplinary work between Earth sciences and environmental policy.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Geology, plate tectonics, rock cycles, and earth materials
- Atmospheric sciences, weather patterns, and climate systems
- Ocean currents, marine ecosystems, and hydrology
- Environmental degradation, conservation biology, and restoration ecology
- Sustainable development, renewable energy, and resource stewardship
- Climate change science and mitigation strategies
- Human impact analysis and environmental policy frameworks

This hub connects to `[[core/science]]`, and often overlaps with `[[parent/biology]]`, `[[core/politics]]`, and `[[core/health]]` when considering socio-political or health-related aspects of environmental issues.
