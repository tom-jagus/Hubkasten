# Home & Environment

## Description

The **Home & Environment** hub captures knowledge and personal practices related to how you organize, design, maintain, and experience the physical space you live in — including your home, workspace, and immediate surroundings.

This includes interior design, decluttering, cleaning routines, home improvement projects, furniture and layout optimization, environmental comfort, sensory experience, and storage solutions.  
It also covers mental associations with space (e.g., calm vs clutter), seasonal adjustments, and routines for maintaining a functional, restorative environment.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Room-by-room organization systems and improvement plans
- Decluttering, minimalism, and storage strategies
- Cleaning routines, schedules, and tools
- Decor themes, lighting, textures, and furniture placement
- Smart home tools and workflow enhancements
- Seasonal setup routines (e.g., spring refresh, winterization)
- Psychological impact of space, air, light, and noise

This hub connects to `[[core/lifestyle]]` and may intersect with `[[core/health]]` (for air quality, ergonomics), `[[core/workspace]]` (for home office environments), and `[[parent/daily habits & routines]]` (for tidiness and upkeep rituals).
