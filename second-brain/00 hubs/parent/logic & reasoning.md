# Logic & Reasoning

## Description

The **Logic & Reasoning** hub captures knowledge about formal and informal systems of thought used to assess arguments, identify fallacies, and evaluate the structure and validity of claims.

This includes propositional and predicate logic, deductive and inductive reasoning, informal logic, critical thinking, argument mapping, and the identification of cognitive or rhetorical fallacies.  
It also provides foundational tools for rigorous thinking in fields like mathematics, computer science, legal theory, and public discourse.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Logical operators, truth tables, and inference rules
- Deductive, inductive, and abductive reasoning
- Validity, soundness, and structure of arguments
- Informal logic: analogies, heuristics, and everyday reasoning
- Common logical fallacies (e.g., strawman, ad hominem, slippery slope)
- Argument reconstruction and evaluation strategies
- Applications of logic in language, law, philosophy, and technology

This hub connects to `[[core/philosophy]]` and often intersects with `[[parent/knowledge & belief]]`, `[[core/science]]`, and `[[core/technology]]` where logic underpins reasoning in both theory and practice.
