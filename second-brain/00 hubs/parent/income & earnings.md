# Income & Earnings

## Description

The **Income & Earnings** hub captures knowledge, tracking, and strategy related to active and passive sources of income — including salary, freelance work, side hustles, royalties, and other cash-generating activities.

This includes pay negotiations, raise history, recurring revenue streams, contract work, affiliate income, creative monetization, and hourly vs project-based compensation models.  
It also supports experimentation with new income streams, long-term income growth strategies, and financial independence tracking.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Job income, pay raises, bonus structures, and benefits
- Freelance or consulting work logs, contracts, and invoices
- Passive income from royalties, investments, or digital products
- Side hustle tracking and evaluation
- Affiliate marketing or business revenue notes
- Diversification strategies and earnings breakdowns
- Income visualization, forecasting, and milestone tracking

This hub connects to `[[core/finances]]` and often overlaps with `[[parent/budgeting & expense tracking]]`, `[[parent/taxes & filing]]`, and `[[parent/financial planning & goals]]`.
