# Renting & Tenancy

## Description

The **Renting & Tenancy** hub captures knowledge and strategies related to living in rented properties, navigating landlord-tenant relationships, lease agreements, and transitioning between rentals.

This includes apartment hunting, application prep, lease negotiation, deposit tracking, maintenance coordination, renter’s rights, and managing roommate dynamics.  
It also covers moving between rentals, handling disputes, understanding regional rental laws, and optimizing rented living spaces.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Rental search strategies and apartment evaluation checklists
- Application prep, income requirements, and background checks
- Lease terms, renewals, break clauses, and subletting policies
- Landlord communication, dispute resolution, and documentation
- Tenant rights and local housing laws
- Roommate agreements, shared responsibilities, and etiquette
- Tips for customizing or organizing rental spaces without damage

This hub connects to `[[core/housing]]` and may overlap with `[[parent/legal & regulatory matters]]`, `[[parent/moving & relocation]]`, and `[[core/lifestyle]]` where day-to-day living is impacted.
