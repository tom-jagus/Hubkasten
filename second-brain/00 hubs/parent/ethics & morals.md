# Ethics & Morals

## Description

The **Ethics & Morals** hub captures knowledge about how individuals, societies, and traditions define right and wrong, evaluate actions, and build systems of moral judgment.

This includes normative ethics (what we ought to do), applied ethics (e.g. bioethics, tech ethics), meta-ethics (the nature of moral facts), and moral psychology (why we behave ethically).  
It also covers ethical frameworks like deontology, consequentialism, virtue ethics, relativism, and care ethics — as well as debates around justice, rights, responsibility, and fairness.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Major ethical theories and their philosophical justifications
- Moral dilemmas, case studies, and real-world ethical challenges
- Historical and cultural variations in moral codes
- Theories of justice, fairness, responsibility, and obligation
- Ethics in specific contexts (AI, medicine, war, environment, economics)
- Relationships between moral values, emotion, and reason
- The role of moral development and social norms in shaping behavior

This hub connects to `[[core/philosophy]]`, and often overlaps with `[[core/politics]]`, `[[core/health]]`, `[[core/technology]]`, or `[[core/culture]]` where ethical questions arise in applied domains.
