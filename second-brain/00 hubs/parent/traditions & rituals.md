# Traditions & Rituals

## Description

The **Traditions & Rituals** hub captures repeated cultural practices that hold symbolic, historical, or communal meaning — including holidays, life-cycle ceremonies, regional customs, and everyday rituals tied to identity and values.

This includes religious and secular rituals, seasonal celebrations, rites of passage, superstitions, initiation ceremonies, family customs, and holiday routines.  
It also supports reflections on how rituals evolve, their role in identity formation, and how tradition connects individuals to collective memory.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- National or religious holidays and their significance
- Birth, marriage, and death ceremonies across cultures
- Regional or ethnic customs and folklore practices
- Family or personal traditions maintained over time
- Notes on the evolution, loss, or revival of rituals
- Symbolic meaning of ritual gestures, foods, or attire
- Comparison of similar rituals across cultural boundaries

This hub connects to `[[core/culture]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/cultural identity & heritage]]`, `[[parent/religion & spirituality]]`, or `[[parent/language & symbols]]`.
