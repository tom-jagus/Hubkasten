# Metaphysics

## Description

The **Metaphysics** hub captures knowledge about the most fundamental aspects of reality — including the nature of existence, identity, objects, time, space, causality, possibility, and the structure of the universe.

This includes classical metaphysical questions such as "What is being?", "What is time?", "Do abstract objects exist?", as well as more modern metaphysical topics such as ontology, modal logic, determinism, and metaphysical realism vs anti-realism.  
It also intersects with cosmology, philosophy of mind, and theology when exploring concepts like the self, the universe, or the divine.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Theories of existence, substance, and identity over time
- Causality, free will vs determinism, and the nature of change
- Time and space as metaphysical or physical constructs
- Ontology: what kinds of things exist and how they are categorized
- Modal metaphysics: necessity, possibility, and possible worlds
- Questions about universals, particulars, and abstract entities
- Cosmological and theological metaphysical arguments

This hub connects to `[[core/philosophy]]`, and may intersect with `[[parent/knowledge & belief]]`, `[[parent/mind & consciousness]]`, or even `[[core/science]]` when dealing with cosmological or physical models of reality.
