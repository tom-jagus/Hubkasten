# Language & Symbols

## Description

The **Language & Symbols** hub captures the systems through which cultures express, encode, and transmit meaning — including spoken and written language, idioms, metaphors, symbolism, and semiotic frameworks.

This includes analysis of language in context, metaphorical expressions, body language, signs, flags, gestures, idiomatic phrases, religious or mythological symbolism, and the cultural implications of language structures.  
It also supports reflection on how meaning shifts across languages or how symbolism is interpreted differently in different cultures.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Idioms, sayings, or culturally unique phrases
- Symbols used in religion, politics, design, or folklore
- Semiotics and theories of meaning-making
- Non-verbal communication and cultural gestures
- Visual symbolism in art, film, or architecture
- Translation challenges and shifts in meaning across languages
- Reflections on metaphor, abstraction, or communication across cultures

This hub connects to `[[core/culture]]` and overlaps with `[[parent/religion & spirituality]]`, `[[parent/creative arts]]`, and `[[parent/cross-cultural studies]]`.
