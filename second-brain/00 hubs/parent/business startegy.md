# Business Strategy

## Description

The **Business Strategy** hub captures high-level planning, decision-making models, and competitive positioning frameworks that define how an organization creates value and sustains long-term growth.

This includes strategic models (e.g. SWOT, Porter’s Five Forces, Blue Ocean), innovation strategies, goal-setting frameworks (e.g. OKRs), market entry tactics, and long-term roadmapping.  
It also supports analysis of company missions, pivots, business model evolution, strategic tradeoffs, and leadership-driven planning.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Strategic frameworks and decision-making models
- Competitive analysis and market positioning
- Business model canvases and value proposition design
- Strategic pivots or realignments in case studies
- Long-term roadmaps, goals, and OKRs
- Internal vs external strategy dynamics
- Notes on strategic thinkers, authors, or theories

This hub connects to `[[core/business]]` and may intersect with `[[parent/leadership & management]]`, `[[parent/innovation & disruption]]`, and `[[parent/business operations]]`.
