# Emerging Tech

## Description

The **Emerging Tech** hub explores newly developing, experimental, or fast-evolving technologies that are shaping the future but may not yet be widely adopted or standardized.

This includes fields such as quantum computing, blockchain, augmented and virtual reality (AR/VR), brain-computer interfaces, 3D printing, edge computing, synthetic biology, spatial computing, and next-gen human-computer interaction.  
It also serves as a holding space for speculative or exploratory areas that may eventually mature into their own categories.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Breakthrough technologies and research frontiers
- Quantum logic, quantum programming, and early-access hardware
- Blockchain protocols, decentralized systems, and crypto infrastructure
- Augmented/virtual/mixed reality platforms and spatial design paradigms
- Biotech, synthetic biology, and human enhancement tools
- Experimental interfaces: BCI, voice, gesture, neural, and more
- Reports, whitepapers, or predictions about emerging technological trends

This hub connects to `[[core/technology]]` and may also link to `[[core/science]]` or `[[core/philosophy]]` where speculative or ethical implications are discussed.
