# Biology

## Description

The **Biology** hub captures knowledge about living systems, including their structure, function, growth, evolution, distribution, and interactions.

This includes cell biology, genetics, ecology, evolution, physiology, molecular biology, microbiology, botany, zoology, and biotechnology.  
It also covers modern interdisciplinary fields like synthetic biology, genomics, systems biology, and the application of biological knowledge in health, agriculture, and sustainability.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Cellular structures, organelles, and biochemical pathways
- Genetic principles, DNA/RNA mechanisms, and heredity
- Evolutionary theory, natural selection, and speciation
- Plant biology, animal physiology, and microbial systems
- Ecosystem dynamics, conservation biology, and biodiversity
- Biotechnology innovations and synthetic biology
- Intersections between biology, health, and environmental science

This hub connects to `[[core/science]]`, and naturally overlaps with `[[parent/earth & environment]]`, `[[parent/neuroscience]]`, and `[[core/health]]` when discussing applied biological research.
