# Sleep & Recovery

## Description

The **Sleep & Recovery** hub captures knowledge, strategies, and personal observations related to rest, circadian rhythm, sleep hygiene, and overall physiological and mental recovery.

This includes sleep tracking, bedtime routines, supplements, sleep disorders, nap optimization, wake-up energy levels, and recovery protocols for stress, fatigue, illness, or physical strain.  
It also supports insights from wearables, mindfulness practices, and adjustments to support deep, restorative rest.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Sleep quality logs, duration tracking, and recovery scores
- Bedtime rituals, winding-down routines, and screen use limits
- Morning energy and cognitive readiness observations
- Tools and supplements used to improve rest (e.g. magnesium, blue blockers)
- Light exposure, temperature, and noise management
- Sleep disorders, disturbances, and strategies for improvement
- Connection between sleep and physical or emotional recovery

This hub connects to `[[core/health]]` and may also overlap with `[[parent/mental & emotional health]]`, `[[core/lifestyle]]` (for routines), or `[[parent/physical health]]` (for energy and immune system tracking).
