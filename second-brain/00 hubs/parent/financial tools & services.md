# Financial Tools & Services

## Description

The **Financial Tools & Services** hub captures knowledge, preferences, and evaluations related to the tools, platforms, and institutions used to manage money — including banks, budgeting apps, investment platforms, cards, and advisors.

This includes app comparisons, account setups, service fees, integrations, financial automation, security practices, and notes on financial professionals (e.g. CPAs, planners).  
It also supports documentation of contact details, workflow routines, and reflections on how tech stacks or service providers influence your financial behavior.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Banking apps, checking/savings accounts, and fee structures
- Budgeting tools like YNAB, Notion, spreadsheets, or mobile apps
- Brokerage platforms, wallets, and financial dashboards
- Advisor or accountant contacts and meeting notes
- Automations between tools (e.g. Zapier, bank sync, Google Sheets)
- Tool reviews, upgrades, and migration checklists
- Security practices: password managers, 2FA, alerts

This hub connects to `[[core/finances]]` and may intersect with `[[parent/budgeting & expense tracking]]`, `[[parent/investing & wealth building]]`, and `[[core/workspace]]` if you include workflow automation.
