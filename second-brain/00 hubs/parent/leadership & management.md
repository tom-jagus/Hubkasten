# Leadership & Management

## Description

The **Leadership & Management** hub captures principles, frameworks, and reflections related to building, guiding, and sustaining effective teams and organizations.

This includes leadership styles, management theory, team building, hiring processes, organizational design, conflict resolution, performance management, and executive decision-making.  
It also supports notes on mentorship, company culture, internal communication, and psychological safety.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Leadership frameworks (e.g. servant leadership, situational leadership)
- Team dynamics, collaboration strategies, and conflict management
- Organizational charts, role clarity, and accountability systems
- Recruitment, onboarding, and performance review processes
- Culture-building practices and company values
- Reflections on past leadership experiences or decisions
- Case studies on great or failed leadership moments

This hub connects to `[[core/business]]` and frequently overlaps with `[[parent/business strategy]]`, `[[parent/business operations]]`, and `[[parent/sales & customer relations]]`.
