# Business Operations

## Description

The **Business Operations** hub captures the day-to-day systems, processes, and logistics that enable a business to deliver value efficiently and sustainably.

This includes workflows, SOPs (standard operating procedures), budgeting, procurement, inventory, internal tooling, performance tracking, and operational optimization.  
It also supports documentation of recurring processes, interdepartmental coordination, vendor management, and scaling infrastructure.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Operational workflows, automations, and efficiency tactics
- Internal dashboards, KPIs, and metrics tracking
- Business tools, platform configurations, and process documentation
- Supply chain logistics, vendors, and procurement strategies
- Scheduling, reporting, and resource allocation
- Budget planning, forecasting, and cash flow management
- Maintenance of operational standards during growth or change

This hub connects to `[[core/business]]` and often intersects with `[[parent/leadership & management]]`, `[[parent/sales & customer relations]]`, and `[[parent/innovation & disruption]]`.
