# Taxes & Filing

## Description

The **Taxes & Filing** hub captures knowledge, documents, and workflows related to preparing, organizing, and optimizing taxes — including income tax, self-employment tax, deductions, and compliance requirements.

This includes annual filing checklists, expense categorization, tax forms, estimated tax payments, accountant communication, and documentation storage.  
It also covers tax-advantaged accounts (e.g. IRAs, HSAs), tax planning for future years, digital vs paper workflows, and jurisdiction-specific obligations.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Filing history, tax prep checklists, and yearly summaries
- Deduction tracking, eligible expenses, and receipts
- W-2s, 1099s, invoices, and supporting documents
- Tax filing tools, software, or CPA contact logs
- Estimated tax payments and quarterly deadlines
- Tax-advantaged strategies and timing of income
- Lessons learned from past audits, mistakes, or changes

This hub connects to `[[core/finances]]` and overlaps with `[[parent/income & earnings]]`, `[[parent/debt & liabilities]]`, and `[[core/workspace]]` if business-related income or filing applies.
