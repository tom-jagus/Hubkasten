# Cybersecurity

## Description

The **Cybersecurity** hub focuses on the protection of digital systems, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, disruption, exploitation, or destruction.

This includes knowledge about security best practices, threat modeling, encryption, secure protocols, authentication systems, vulnerability management, penetration testing, incident response, and risk assessment.  
It also covers topics related to privacy, compliance, secure software development, and the evolving landscape of cyber threats in both personal and organizational contexts.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Security principles and architecture (confidentiality, integrity, availability)
- Encryption, authentication, and access control methods
- Network and cloud security practices
- Web application vulnerabilities (e.g., OWASP Top 10)
- Penetration testing, ethical hacking, and red/blue team methods
- Risk management, threat modeling, and compliance frameworks (e.g., GDPR, SOC 2)
- Tools and platforms for auditing, monitoring, and protecting systems

This hub is linked to `[[core/technology]]`, and may connect to `[[parent/software engineering]]`, `[[parent/devops & infrastructure]]`, or even `[[core/politics]]` when discussing digital rights, cyberwarfare, or state-level regulations.
