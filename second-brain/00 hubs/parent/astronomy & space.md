# Astronomy & Space

## Description

The **Astronomy & Space** hub captures knowledge about celestial objects, cosmic phenomena, and the large-scale structure and history of the universe.

This includes observational and theoretical astronomy, astrophysics, cosmology, planetary science, space exploration, spaceflight engineering, and the study of phenomena such as black holes, supernovae, exoplanets, and dark matter.  
It also addresses the intersection between astronomy and physics, as well as the role of data science and simulation in modeling space systems.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Stars, galaxies, planetary systems, and celestial mechanics
- The Big Bang theory, cosmic microwave background, and cosmological models
- Tools and techniques for astronomical observation (telescopes, space probes)
- Astrophysics principles, gravitational theory, and orbital dynamics
- Spaceflight technology, mission design, and agencies (NASA, ESA, etc.)
- Exploration of exoplanets, habitability, and astrobiology
- Philosophical and scientific implications of humanity’s place in the cosmos

This hub connects to `[[core/science]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/physics]]`, `[[core/philosophy]]`, and `[[core/technology]]` in the context of space engineering or metaphysical reflections.
