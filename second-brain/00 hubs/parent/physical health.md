# Physical Health

## Description

The **Physical Health** hub captures knowledge, observations, and decisions related to the general functioning of the body — including signs, symptoms, metrics, and strategies for maintaining or improving well-being.

This includes energy levels, aches and pains, mobility, inflammation, digestion, immune health, illness recovery, and body awareness that falls outside the domain of structured fitness or diagnosed medical conditions.  
It provides space for body literacy, health observations, early symptom tracking, physical maintenance habits, and experiments with recovery techniques or lifestyle adjustments.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Observations on general vitality, fatigue, or pain patterns
- Body systems: cardiovascular, digestive, immune, endocrine, etc.
- Non-emergency symptoms and early warning signs
- Health metrics: weight, blood pressure, temperature, HRV, etc.
- Illness recovery logs (colds, flu, infections)
- Body awareness and physical sensations (e.g. bloating, dizziness)
- Long-term reflections on physical resilience and change

This hub connects to `[[core/health]]` and may intersect with `[[parent/nutrition & diet]]`, `[[parent/preventive care & checkups]]`, and `[[parent/medical history & conditions]]`.
