# Chemistry

## Description

The **Chemistry** hub captures knowledge about the composition, properties, transformations, and interactions of matter at the atomic and molecular levels.

This includes organic, inorganic, physical, analytical, and biochemistry, as well as chemical bonding, reaction mechanisms, thermochemistry, material science, and spectroscopy.  
It also covers laboratory methods, safety practices, and applications of chemistry in fields such as medicine, energy, manufacturing, and environmental science.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Atomic theory, chemical bonding, and molecular structure
- Periodic table principles and elemental properties
- Organic chemistry reactions, synthesis, and biomolecules
- Inorganic chemistry, coordination compounds, and crystal structures
- Physical chemistry: thermodynamics, kinetics, quantum chemistry
- Analytical techniques (chromatography, spectroscopy, titration)
- Applications of chemistry in technology, health, and sustainability

This hub connects to `[[core/science]]`, and may overlap with `[[parent/biology]]`, `[[parent/earth & environment]]`, or `[[core/technology]]` depending on interdisciplinary contexts.
