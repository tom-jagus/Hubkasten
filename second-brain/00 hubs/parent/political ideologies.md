# Political Ideologies

## Description

The **Political Ideologies** hub captures knowledge about the systems of thought and belief that guide political values, policies, and institutional design across cultures and eras.

This includes ideologies such as liberalism, conservatism, socialism, communism, nationalism, fascism, anarchism, environmentalism, and populism.  
It also covers the philosophical roots of these systems, their historical evolution, internal branches, and influence on political parties, leaders, and global movements.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Core political ideologies and their defining beliefs, goals, and critiques
- Variants and hybrids (e.g., neoliberalism, democratic socialism, eco-fascism)
- Historical development and ideological shifts across time and regions
- Influence of ideologies on policy, governance, and public discourse
- Political spectrums (left–right, authoritarian–libertarian) and typologies
- Role of ideology in voter behavior, identity politics, and polarization
- Intersections with religion, economics, and culture

This hub connects to `[[core/politics]]`, and may intersect with `[[core/philosophy]]`, `[[parent/law & constitutional frameworks]]`, and `[[parent/political systems]]` when ideologies shape or challenge legal and institutional structures.
