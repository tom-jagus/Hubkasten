# Study Tools

## Description

The **Study Tools** hub captures the techniques, technologies, and analog methods used to enhance active learning, memory retention, and comprehension.

This includes digital apps, flashcard systems, analog techniques, highlighting styles, mind mapping tools, annotation workflows, and spaced repetition systems.  
It also supports reviews of study efficiency, learning aids, and experimentation with workflows for mastering complex subjects.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Flashcard tools (e.g. Anki), setup templates, and decks
- Mind mapping, whiteboarding, and visual study tools
- Reading and annotation workflows (e.g. Readwise, PDF markup)
- Highlighting methods and review systems
- Study planner templates or goal-setting apps
- Environment optimization (e.g. Pomodoro, focus tools)
- Reflections on effectiveness of different study aids

This hub connects to `[[core/education]]` and closely overlaps with `[[parent/learning methods & systems]]`, `[[parent/online learning & courses]]`, and `[[core/workspace]]` when workflow tools are involved.
