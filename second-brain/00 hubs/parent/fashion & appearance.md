# Fashion & Appearance

## Description

The **Fashion & Appearance** hub captures knowledge, inspiration, and personal strategy related to clothing, grooming, style, and the external expression of identity, confidence, and culture.

This includes wardrobes, outfit planning, grooming routines, personal style development, fashion principles, color theory, seasonal updates, and notes on aesthetics, branding, or social signaling through appearance.  
It also provides space to explore self-presentation, material quality, fashion history, and how dress affects mindset and perception.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Personal style goals, principles, and inspiration boards
- Outfit planning for events, seasons, or daily use
- Grooming, skincare, haircare, and scent routines
- Fabric types, fit guides, tailoring, and clothing care
- Fashion influences from culture, subcultures, or history
- Capsule wardrobe design and minimalism approaches
- Relationship between appearance, mood, and self-perception

This hub connects to `[[core/lifestyle]]` and may also intersect with `[[core/culture]]` (for aesthetic traditions), `[[parent/social life & relationships]]` (presentation in context), or `[[core/workspace]]` (for professional attire).
