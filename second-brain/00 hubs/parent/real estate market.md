# Real Estate Market

## Description

The **Real Estate Market** hub captures knowledge and insights about how property markets function — including pricing trends, regional comparisons, buyer/seller dynamics, economic influences, and long-term valuation patterns.

This includes local and national housing trends, neighborhood profiles, supply/demand shifts, real estate cycles, rental vs ownership conditions, and investment potential.  
It also supports tracking market conditions over time, comparing locations, analyzing economic indicators, and making informed real estate decisions.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Housing market trends and pricing changes in different regions
- Interest rate effects, inflation, and affordability metrics
- Location scouting and neighborhood comparisons
- Urban vs rural development patterns
- Buyer psychology and demand forecasting
- Timing strategies and cyclical analysis
- Real estate data sources and visualization tools

This hub connects to `[[core/housing]]` and may overlap with `[[core/finances]]` (for real estate investing), `[[parent/buying & selling property]]`, and `[[parent/renting & tenancy]]`.
