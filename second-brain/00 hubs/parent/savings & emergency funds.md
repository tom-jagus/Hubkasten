# Savings & Emergency Funds

## Description

The **Savings & Emergency Funds** hub captures knowledge, goals, and progress tracking related to short-term financial resilience — including emergency funds, planned purchases, sinking funds, and liquidity strategy.

This includes high-yield savings accounts, emergency fund rules-of-thumb, short-term savings goals (e.g. vacations, appliances), and decision-making around how much to keep in cash or near-cash assets.  
It also supports reflection on saving psychology, tradeoffs between spending and security, and habits for automating or motivating consistent savings.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Emergency fund strategies, milestones, and buffers
- Targeted savings goals and timelines (e.g. travel, medical, gifts)
- Sinking funds by category or account
- Bank account structure and liquidity planning
- Automation techniques for saving consistently
- Savings motivation, visualizations, and behavior tracking
- Savings tools and account comparisons (e.g. HYSA, credit union, fintech)

This hub connects to `[[core/finances]]` and overlaps with `[[parent/budgeting & expense tracking]]` (for allocation), `[[parent/financial planning & goals]]`, and `[[core/housing]]` (for large home-related expenses).
