# Business Laws & Ethics

## Description

The **Business Laws & Ethics** hub captures legal regulations, compliance practices, and ethical decision-making frameworks that govern how businesses operate within society.

This includes contracts, intellectual property, employment law, corporate governance, data protection, antitrust issues, and international regulations.  
It also supports reflection on ethical dilemmas, corporate social responsibility, whistleblowing, sustainability mandates, and values-driven leadership.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Business contracts, licensing, and intellectual property rights
- Employment law, worker rights, and labor compliance
- Privacy policies, GDPR, and data handling regulations
- Ethical theories and their application in business decisions
- Regulatory bodies and industry-specific requirements
- Notes from legal advice, compliance training, or case law
- Corporate responsibility, ESG frameworks, and sustainability ethics

This hub connects to `[[core/business]]` and may also intersect with `[[core/politics]]`, `[[parent/leadership & management]]`, or `[[parent/finance & operations]]` in compliance-heavy environments.
