# Scientific Method & Research Principles

## Description

The **Scientific Method & Research Principles** hub captures knowledge about the processes, frameworks, standards, and ethics that govern how scientific inquiry is conducted across disciplines.

This includes hypothesis generation, experimental design, reproducibility, falsifiability, data analysis, peer review, publication standards, open science movements, and the evolving methodologies used to ensure the credibility and rigor of research.  
It also addresses research ethics, responsible data handling, and critical thinking applied to the evaluation of scientific claims.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Steps of the scientific method: observation, hypothesis, experimentation, conclusion
- Experimental design strategies, control groups, bias reduction
- Statistical analysis, data integrity, and replication
- Peer review processes, scientific publishing, and academic communication
- Open science initiatives, preprints, and collaborative research platforms
- Ethical considerations in human/animal research and data privacy
- Fallacies, cognitive biases, and evaluating scientific reliability

This hub connects to `[[core/science]]` and may also link to `[[core/philosophy]]` when discussing the philosophy of science and knowledge generation.
