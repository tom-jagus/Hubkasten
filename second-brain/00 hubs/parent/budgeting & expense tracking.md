# Budgeting & Expense Tracking

## Description

The **Budgeting & Expense Tracking** hub captures knowledge, habits, and tools related to managing everyday financial flow — including income allocation, spending awareness, savings habits, and lifestyle cost optimization.

This includes zero-based budgeting, category planning, envelope systems, transaction logs, subscriptions, expense analysis, spending audits, and calendar-based budgeting routines.  
It also supports monthly or quarterly reviews, intentional spending philosophies (e.g. frugality, minimalism), and the use of personal finance apps or spreadsheets.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Budget templates, methods, and personal experiments
- Expense categories, recurring costs, and spending priorities
- Monthly reviews, savings rate analysis, and cash flow charts
- Subscription management and service cost comparisons
- Spending challenges or resets (e.g. no-spend months)
- Journaling reflections on money habits and emotions
- Tools like YNAB, Excel, Notion, or budgeting apps

This hub connects to `[[core/finances]]` and may overlap with `[[parent/savings & emergency funds]]`, `[[parent/financial planning & goals]]`, and `[[core/lifestyle]]` depending on the level of intentionality applied.
