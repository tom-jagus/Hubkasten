# Investing & Wealth Building

## Description

The **Investing & Wealth Building** hub captures knowledge, strategies, tools, and reflections related to building long-term financial assets and achieving financial independence.

This includes traditional investing (e.g. stocks, ETFs, mutual funds), real estate, crypto, retirement accounts, risk management, asset allocation, and compounding.  
It also covers behavioral investing psychology, investment research, and personal strategy building — from index fund simplicity to advanced multi-asset portfolios.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Investment philosophies and portfolio allocation strategies
- Passive vs active investing, dollar-cost averaging, rebalancing
- Retirement accounts (e.g. IRAs, 401(k)s, pensions)
- Risk tolerance, volatility response, and time horizon planning
- Real estate investing and rental income strategy
- Crypto or alternative investments (e.g. collectibles, businesses)
- Tools, brokers, dashboards, or tax implications of investing

This hub connects to `[[core/finances]]` and may intersect with `[[parent/financial planning & goals]]`, `[[core/housing]]` (for real estate investments), or `[[parent/taxes & filing]]`.
