# Hobbies & Recreation

## Description

The **Hobbies & Recreation** hub captures knowledge, ideas, and reflections related to passion projects, hands-on creativity, skill-building, collecting, and time spent in enjoyable or restorative non-work activities.

This includes creative outlets (e.g. writing, drawing, music), collecting (e.g. books, games, tools), strategic games, nature activities, crafting, home projects, and personal challenges like learning a language or building something from scratch.  
It also embraces intentional play, free time structure, and the mental, emotional, or physical benefits of having meaningful hobbies.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Favorite hobbies, their goals, and seasonal variations
- Gear, setups, guides, or routines for creative activities
- Skill acquisition: progress tracking, courses, or challenges
- Hobby journaling and reflections on time well spent
- Collecting systems or personal archives (e.g. books, games, tools)
- Community aspects of hobbies (forums, clubs, shared spaces)
- Mindful recreation, play theory, or relaxation techniques

This hub connects to `[[core/lifestyle]]`, and may also intersect with `[[parent/travel & leisure]]`, `[[core/workspace]]` (when skills relate to career), and `[[core/creativity]]` if you choose to expand the creative domain later.
