# Innovation & Disruption

## Description

The **Innovation & Disruption** hub captures frameworks, trends, and case studies related to how businesses create new value, challenge incumbents, and adapt to changing markets through transformation and invention.

This includes product innovation, R&D, disruptive technologies, market shifts, creative strategy, innovation pipelines, and breakthrough thinking.  
It also supports tracking of innovation methodologies (e.g. design thinking), venture pivots, innovation culture, and forecasting future trends.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Case studies of disruptive companies and business models
- Notes on emerging technologies and industries (e.g. AI, biotech, blockchain)
- Product development cycles and innovation processes
- Innovation frameworks like design thinking or jobs-to-be-done
- Organizational innovation culture and intrapreneurship
- Trendwatching and forecasting tools
- Strategic transformation in legacy companies

This hub connects to `[[core/business]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/business strategy]]`, `[[core/technology]]`, and `[[parent/entrepreneurship & startups]]`.
