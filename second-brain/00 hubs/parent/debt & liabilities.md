# Debt & Liabilities

## Description

The **Debt & Liabilities** hub captures knowledge, logs, and strategies related to personal or household debt — including mortgages, loans, credit cards, and other financial obligations.

This includes debt payoff methods (e.g. avalanche, snowball), interest rate analysis, payment plans, refinancing, credit reports, and liability tracking across multiple accounts.  
It also supports behavioral and emotional reflection, such as mindset shifts around debt, planning for large purchases, and balancing liability reduction with investing.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Mortgage, auto, student, or personal loan details
- Credit card balances, APRs, and payoff schedules
- Debt repayment strategies and milestone tracking
- Interest vs principal breakdowns
- Refinancing research and offers
- Credit score monitoring and improvement tactics
- Psychological reflections on debt and financial stress

This hub connects to `[[core/finances]]` and often intersects with `[[parent/taxes & filing]]`, `[[core/housing]]` (for mortgages), and `[[parent/financial planning & goals]]`.
