# Social Life & Relationships

## Description

The **Social Life & Relationships** hub captures knowledge, reflections, and systems related to human connection — including friendships, dating, family bonds, social behavior, communication, and emotional intelligence.

This includes relationship-building strategies, meaningful conversations, conflict resolution, rituals of connection, support systems, social planning, group dynamics, and evolving interpersonal roles.  
It also covers boundary-setting, relationship journaling, trust development, long-distance dynamics, love languages, and how relationships shape your lifestyle and emotional well-being.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Friendship cultivation, deep conversations, and connection rituals
- Dating experiences, partner compatibility, and long-term relationship insights
- Family systems, boundaries, support roles, and communication patterns
- Social calendar planning and event reflection
- Relationship psychology, attachment styles, and emotional literacy
- Journaling about relationships, tensions, milestones, or gratitude
- Digital social life, texting habits, online/offline dynamics

This hub connects to `[[core/lifestyle]]`, and may intersect with `[[core/health]]` (for emotional health), `[[parent/fashion & appearance]]` (for self-presentation), or `[[core/philosophy]]` (for ethical and existential reflections on connection).
