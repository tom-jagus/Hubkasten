# Medical History & Conditions

## Description

The **Medical History & Conditions** hub captures detailed records, notes, and timelines related to personal diagnoses, medical events, chronic conditions, and treatment histories.

This includes symptom onset and progression, specialist referrals, medications, procedures, diagnoses, treatment outcomes, flare-ups, relapses, and ongoing care plans.  
It also supports building a longitudinal view of your health journey, organizing documentation, and maintaining communication logs with healthcare providers.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Chronological logs of diagnoses, symptoms, or hospital visits
- Treatment plans, medication changes, or outcome tracking
- Notes from medical appointments or second opinions
- Care plans for chronic conditions (e.g. asthma, migraines, autoimmune)
- Pain logs, relapse triggers, or daily symptom tracking
- Relevant family medical history or genetic conditions
- Summary documents for sharing with providers

This hub connects to `[[core/health]]` and overlaps heavily with `[[parent/preventive care & checkups]]`, `[[parent/physical health]]`, and `[[parent/healthcare system & insurance]]`.
