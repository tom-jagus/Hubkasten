# Marketing & Branding

## Description

The **Marketing & Branding** hub captures strategies, frameworks, and creative work that help businesses attract, engage, and retain customers through messaging, identity, and promotion.

This includes market segmentation, customer personas, campaign design, content marketing, SEO, positioning, tone of voice, visual identity, and branding strategy.  
It also supports experimentation, performance analytics, storytelling, community building, and brand equity development.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Target audience research, segmentation, and personas
- Marketing funnels, campaign ideas, and ad performance
- Content calendars, email strategy, and channel optimization
- Brand voice, logo design, typography, and style guides
- Marketing psychology, storytelling, and emotional triggers
- Social media tactics and platform-specific strategies
- Notes on successful brands, campaigns, or creators

This hub connects to `[[core/business]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/sales & customer relations]]`, `[[core/creativity]]`, or `[[parent/innovation & disruption]]`.
