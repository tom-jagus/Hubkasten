# Mortgages & Financing

## Description

The **Mortgages & Financing** hub captures knowledge, planning tools, and decision-making strategies related to funding property purchases, managing debt, comparing loan products, and budgeting for long-term affordability.

This includes pre-approval steps, credit readiness, mortgage types, interest rates, amortization, refinancing, down payment strategies, and lender comparisons.  
It also covers affordability calculators, payment schedules, financial trade-offs, and integration of housing costs into broader personal finance systems.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Fixed vs variable rates, mortgage terms, and lender conditions
- Pre-approval checklists and credit score preparation
- Loan comparison tools, broker experiences, and financial modeling
- Down payment savings plans and affordability goals
- Amortization breakdowns, extra payments, and payoff planning
- Refinancing scenarios and timing considerations
- Integration of housing debt into net worth tracking or investment strategy

This hub connects to `[[core/housing]]`, and closely intersects with `[[core/finances]]`, `[[parent/buying & selling property]]`, and `[[parent/legal & regulatory matters]]`.
