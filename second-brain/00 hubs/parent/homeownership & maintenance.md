# Homeownership & Maintenance

## Description

The **Homeownership & Maintenance** hub captures knowledge and responsibilities associated with owning a home, including ongoing care, repairs, upgrades, and long-term property value management.

This includes utility management, seasonal maintenance, contractor coordination, warranties, inspections, DIY improvements, energy efficiency, and home recordkeeping.  
It also covers homeownership routines, budgeting for upkeep, learning to troubleshoot systems, and the emotional or lifestyle impact of being a homeowner.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Home maintenance checklists (monthly, seasonal, annual)
- HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and appliance servicing
- Contractor selection, quotes, and project logs
- DIY tools, techniques, safety, and successes/failures
- Homeownership costs and recurring budgeting
- Renovation goals and project planning
- Reflections on living with repairs, routines, and property growth

This hub connects to `[[core/housing]]` and may intersect with `[[core/lifestyle]]`, `[[core/finances]]`, and `[[parent/legal & regulatory matters]]` depending on the nature of upgrades, compliance, and home investments.
