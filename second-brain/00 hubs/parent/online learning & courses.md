# Online Learning & Courses

## Description

The **Online Learning & Courses** hub captures digital education formats, platforms, and notes from structured online content — including MOOCs, asynchronous learning, video lectures, bootcamps, and certification programs.

This includes course outlines, in-progress learning logs, platform comparisons (e.g. Coursera, Udemy, edX), notes taken from online content, self-paced study flows, and reflections on engagement or course quality.  
It also supports long-term planning for certificates, reskilling, and building custom online curricula.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Enrolled or completed online courses and certifications
- Learning platform evaluations and user experience
- Lecture notes, assignments, and feedback logs
- Self-paced learning plans and study workflows
- Personal reflections on engagement, retention, or pacing
- Certificate or badge tracking for career development
- Course wishlists or reviews

This hub connects to `[[core/education]]` and may overlap with `[[parent/study tools]]`, `[[parent/schooling & achievements]]`, or `[[parent/learning methods & systems]]` for managing digital study habits.
