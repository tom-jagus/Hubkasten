# Political Communication

## Description

The **Political Communication** hub captures knowledge about the ways information is crafted, disseminated, interpreted, and leveraged to influence public opinion and political outcomes.

This includes political rhetoric, campaign messaging, media relations, public debates, speechwriting, political advertising, digital influence operations, propaganda analysis, and crisis communication.  
It also covers the evolution of political communication through traditional media (TV, radio, print) and new media (social platforms, influencers, microtargeting).

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Political speech strategies, narrative framing, and messaging tactics
- Election campaign advertisements, slogans, and communication plans
- Press conferences, interviews, debates, and public addresses
- Media influence on political perception and behavior
- Disinformation, misinformation, propaganda, and censorship practices
- Social media dynamics, political influencers, algorithmic amplification
- Psychological effects of communication strategies (fear appeals, emotional framing)

This hub connects to `[[core/politics]]`, and may also intersect with `[[parent/political participation]]`, `[[parent/geopolitics & international relations]]`, and `[[core/culture]]` where media and public discourse shape collective political realities.
