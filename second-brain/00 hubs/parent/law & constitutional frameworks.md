# Law & Constitutional Frameworks

## Description

The **Law & Constitutional Frameworks** hub captures knowledge about the legal structures and principles that define the organization, authority, and limitations of political systems and institutions.

This includes constitutions, legal codes, judicial systems, rule of law principles, civil rights, administrative law, international law, constitutional amendments, and the interpretation of legal texts over time.  
It also covers theories of constitutionalism, separation of powers, checks and balances, and the relationship between law and society.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Different types of constitutions (written, unwritten, rigid, flexible)
- The development and structure of legal systems (common law, civil law, religious law)
- Judicial review and constitutional courts
- Civil liberties, human rights, and legal protections for citizens
- Amendments, legal reforms, and the evolution of constitutional documents
- International law and supranational legal frameworks (e.g., UN, EU law)
- Legal theory and philosophy (e.g., natural law vs positive law)

This hub connects to `[[core/politics]]` and often overlaps with `[[parent/political systems]]`, `[[parent/public policy & administration]]`, and `[[parent/political ideologies]]` where legal principles shape or constrain governance.
