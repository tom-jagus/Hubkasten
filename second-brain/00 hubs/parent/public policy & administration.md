# Public Policy & Administration

## Description

The **Public Policy & Administration** hub captures knowledge about how governments and public institutions design, implement, manage, and evaluate laws, regulations, and public services.

This includes policymaking processes, public sector management, bureaucracy structures, regulatory frameworks, fiscal policy, social programs, public service delivery, and theories of governance efficiency and accountability.  
It also covers the practical challenges of translating political goals into effective actions and services for citizens.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Stages of policymaking: agenda-setting, formulation, adoption, implementation, evaluation
- Public administration theories and models (e.g., Weberian bureaucracy, New Public Management)
- Budgeting, taxation, and fiscal policy management
- Welfare programs, education policy, healthcare administration, environmental regulation
- Regulatory bodies and oversight mechanisms
- Intergovernmental relations (national, regional, municipal cooperation)
- Case studies of public policy successes and failures

This hub connects to `[[core/politics]]` and naturally overlaps with `[[parent/law & constitutional frameworks]]`, `[[parent/political ideologies]]`, and `[[parent/political participation]]` where policy outcomes are influenced by citizen action or legal structures.
