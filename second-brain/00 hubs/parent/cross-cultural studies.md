# Cross-Cultural Studies

## Description

The **Cross-Cultural Studies** hub captures comparative analysis of cultural systems, behaviors, norms, and communication styles across different societies, time periods, or global regions.

This includes notes on intercultural communication, anthropology, sociology, cultural dimensions theory (e.g. Hofstede, Hall), translation challenges, culture shock, and global ethics.  
It also supports deeper understanding of how values, customs, symbols, and worldviews shape — and differ between — communities around the world.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Comparative analysis of norms (e.g. individualism vs collectivism)
- Notes on etiquette, gestures, or values across cultures
- Intercultural communication theory or case studies
- Global education or work practices and expectations
- Multilingualism and translation between cultural systems
- Cultural psychology, stereotypes, and misunderstanding
- Study abroad reflections or cross-cultural observations

This hub connects to `[[core/culture]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/language & symbols]]`, `[[parent/traditions & rituals]]`, `[[core/politics]]`, or `[[parent/education policies & reforms]]`.
