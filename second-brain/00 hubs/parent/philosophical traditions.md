# Philosophical Traditions

## Description

The **Philosophical Traditions** hub captures knowledge about major historical and cultural schools of thought, their distinctive worldviews, and their influence on ethics, metaphysics, logic, politics, and human nature.

This includes traditions such as Stoicism, Existentialism, Pragmatism, Confucianism, Buddhism, Continental vs Analytic philosophy, and indigenous or non-Western philosophical systems.  
It provides space for understanding philosophy not only through topics or methods, but also through lineages of thinkers, cultural contexts, and intellectual movements.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Core ideas and practices of philosophical traditions (e.g. Stoic virtue, Buddhist non-self)
- Major figures and their historical/philosophical impact
- Comparison of schools: e.g. rationalism vs empiricism, Western vs Eastern thought
- Evolution and internal debates within traditions
- Influence of philosophical traditions on politics, religion, ethics, and culture
- Philosophical movements across time (e.g. Enlightenment, Postmodernism)
- Intersections with art, literature, and theology

This hub connects to `[[core/philosophy]]` and may overlap with `[[parent/ethics & morals]]`, `[[parent/metaphysics]]`, and `[[core/culture]]` depending on the focus of the tradition or its influence.
