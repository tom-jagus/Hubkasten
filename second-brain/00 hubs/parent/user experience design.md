# User Experience Design

## Description

The **User Experience (UX) Design** hub focuses on the research, principles, methods, and tools used to create digital products and systems that are functional, intuitive, accessible, and emotionally engaging for users.

This includes topics such as user research, interaction design (IxD), information architecture (IA), usability testing, accessibility standards (a11y), wireframing, prototyping, design systems, visual communication, and behavioral psychology as applied to digital interfaces.  
It also covers the broader impact of UX design on business outcomes, user retention, product-market fit, and ethical considerations in design.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Principles of effective interface and interaction design
- Usability heuristics and accessibility guidelines
- Methods for conducting user research and usability testing
- Prototyping tools, workflows, and wireframing best practices
- Information architecture patterns and navigation design
- Psychology of user behavior, cognitive load, and decision-making
- Human-computer interaction (HCI) theories and practical applications

This hub links directly to `[[core/technology]]`, and frequently overlaps with `[[parent/web development]]`, `[[parent/software engineering]]`, and occasionally `[[core/business]]` when discussing UX's role in product strategy or market success.
