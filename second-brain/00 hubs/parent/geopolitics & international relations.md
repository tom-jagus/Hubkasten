# Geopolitics & International Relations

## Description

The **Geopolitics & International Relations** hub captures knowledge about how nations interact, compete, cooperate, and exert influence in global affairs.

This includes diplomacy, military strategy, foreign policy, treaties, international organizations (e.g. UN, NATO, WTO), alliances, sanctions, war and peace studies, and the role of geography, economics, and ideology in shaping global power structures.  
It also covers the evolution of international systems, global governance, and theories explaining state behavior on the world stage.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Foreign policy strategies and national interest doctrines
- Military alliances, arms races, and conflict escalation
- Diplomatic theory and negotiation practices
- Geopolitical analysis of regions (e.g., Middle East, Indo-Pacific, Arctic)
- International law, sovereignty, and global governance bodies
- Case studies in diplomacy, war, and peacekeeping missions
- Theories of international relations (realism, liberalism, constructivism, etc.)

This hub connects to `[[core/politics]]` and may also link to `[[core/history]]`, `[[parent/political systems]]`, and `[[parent/political communication]]` when examining state behavior or international propaganda.
