# Pop Culture & Internet

## Description

The **Pop Culture & Internet** hub captures fast-moving digital culture — including viral trends, social media behavior, influencer dynamics, memes, online communities, and the evolving nature of public discourse online.

This includes commentary on platforms (e.g. TikTok, YouTube, Reddit), fandoms, cultural phenomena, internet slang, algorithmic culture, digital ethics, and how culture is created and consumed online.  
It also supports notes about identity performance, subcultures, meme evolution, and the societal impact of internet-native behavior.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Internet trends, viral moments, and meme origins
- Influencer profiles and social media commentary
- Online subcultures, slang, or digital rituals
- Cultural criticism of algorithm-driven platforms
- TikTok vs Instagram vs YouTube: format, tone, and reach
- Fandom culture, shipping, stan behavior, and digital parasociality
- Emotional or societal impact of always-connected environments

This hub connects to `[[core/culture]]` and frequently intersects with `[[parent/media & entertainment]]`, `[[core/lifestyle]]`, and `[[core/technology]]` (for platform mechanics, UX, and algorithm design).
