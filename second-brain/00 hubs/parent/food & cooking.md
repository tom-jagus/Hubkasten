# Food & Cooking

## Description

The **Food & Cooking** hub captures knowledge, preferences, routines, and experiments related to what you eat, how you prepare meals, and how food contributes to your lifestyle, culture, and health.

This includes recipes, meal planning, grocery strategies, cooking techniques, nutritional insights, food rituals, culinary goals, and reflections on food as both necessity and enjoyment.  
It also covers food tracking systems, ingredient substitutions, kitchen tools, and the influence of dietary philosophy (e.g. veganism, intermittent fasting, Mediterranean diet).

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Favorite recipes, cooking methods, or ingredient combinations
- Weekly or seasonal meal plans and grocery templates
- Nutrition principles, macros, and food quality
- Kitchen workflows, tools, storage tips, and optimization
- Cultural food traditions and dining experiences
- Dietary experiments or reflections (e.g. fasting, keto, gluten-free)
- Links to health, energy, and emotional well-being via food

This hub connects to `[[core/lifestyle]]` and may overlap with `[[core/health]]` (for diet tracking or nutrition), `[[parent/daily habits & routines]]`, and `[[core/culture]]` when food intersects with identity and tradition.
