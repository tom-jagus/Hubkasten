# Schooling & Achievements

## Description

The **Schooling & Achievements** hub captures structured educational experiences and personal academic milestones — including schooling history, institutional choices, exams, certificates, grades, and educational goals.

This includes notes about formal education pathways (K–12, higher education, vocational programs), family education plans, academic awards, entrance exams, diplomas, and transcripts.  
It also supports reflections on learning environments, school selection, education tracking for children, and goal-setting for current or future study.

Use this hub to map notes about:

- Educational history for yourself or family members
- Admission strategies, school comparisons, or application notes
- Report cards, exam results, transcript summaries
- Scholarships, honors, certifications, and special recognitions
- Graduation milestones and goal tracking
- Homeschooling frameworks or alternative schooling paths
- Academic reflections or journaling

This hub connects to `[[core/education]]` and may also intersect with `[[parent/education policies & reforms]]`, `[[parent/online learning & courses]]`, or `[[parent/learning methods & systems]]`.
